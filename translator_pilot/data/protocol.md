# CommonDesk translation pilot

The internal corpus contains 35 originally invented civic incidents, with five model-authored language views per incident (English, Thai, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu). Each incident appears in exactly one split. The 25 training, 5 development and 5 held-out incidents expand to 500, 100 and 100 ordered examples. Each direction therefore has only five held-out incidents. No seed fixtures, resident submissions, archive records, photos, human ratings, external corpus or independently validated references enter this corpus. The operational syntax, register and dialect of real resident complaints may differ. Automated fidelity judgments do not establish human review.

Freeze the split files and this protocol before either model trains. Keep held-out sources, candidates, keys and ballots internal. Select hyperparameters, decoding and checkpoints using training/development alone. Run the frozen base and the selected adapter with identical held-out inputs and decoding configuration. Use every output, including empty or wrong-language outputs.

Two independent automated readers each receive shuffled, opaque IDs, the source, its language, the requested target language and an individual candidate. Do not provide model identity, references, another reader's ballots or pairing keys. Ask whether the candidate is in the requested language and preserves the source meaning, allowing idiomatic paraphrases. Return yes or no and one or more short reason tags. A changed hazard, negation, location, number, unit or requested action is a material failure. Mark additions, omissions, wrong language and other material errors. When both readers say yes, count a pass. When both say no, count a failure. Mixed votes are unresolved. Missing or duplicate judgments invalidate a completed tally.

Report raw pass, failure and unresolved counts per direction and condition with paired win/loss/tie counts. No aggregate result can qualify a failing direction. A synthetic pilot candidate may advance to further review only with five of five concordant passes in that direction, no material failure or unresolved judgment, and no regression against the frozen control. Equal quality does not establish an adapter benefit. Any direction with fewer than 100 independently sampled, deployment-representative incidents and appropriately authorized bilingual validation remains ineligible for production. A future pre-registered production test should require zero critical errors, a one-sided 95% lower confidence bound of at least 95% on fidelity per direction, and a separate latency, privacy and operational review. Even zero failures among five independent cases gives only a 54.9% one-sided lower bound. Repeated renderings of one incident cannot count as independent incidents.

Signing uses a freshly generated run-local Ed25519 key. The public key and published manifest digest identify this freeze. A run-local signature is an integrity receipt and makes no assertion of personal identity.
